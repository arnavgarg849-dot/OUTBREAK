Section 12 · Agriculture (14%) & Food Waste Methane

Food Footprint & Zero-Waste Methane Prevention

Roughly one-third of all food produced globally is wasted, generating 8% of global greenhouse gases as it decomposes anaerobically in landfills into methane.

HIGH INTENSITY · 25–60 kg CO₂e / kg food

Ruminant Meat & Hard Cheese

Enteric fermentation and pasture land clearing generate up to 20× more emissions per gram of protein than legumes.

MODERATE · 4–9 kg CO₂e / kg food

Poultry, Eggs & Farmed Fish

Swapping red meat for poultry or sustainable aquaculture cuts meal carbon intensity by over 70% immediately.

LOW INTENSITY · 0.4–2.0 kg CO₂e / kg food

Pulses, Millets, Grains & Vegetables

Nitrogen-fixing lentils, millets, and seasonal produce require minimal water and enrich soil organic carbon.
