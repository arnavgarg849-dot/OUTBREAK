Section 09 · Atmospheric Physics & CO₂e Equivalency

The Science of Greenhouse Gases & Radiative Forcing

Why CO₂ accounts for 74% of global warming while methane (CH₄) and nitrous oxide (N₂O) act as intense short-term heat accelerators—and how CarbonPulse standardizes every gas into kg CO₂e using IPCC AR6 Global Warming Potentials (GWP-100).

CO₂ · Carbon Dioxide GWP = 1×

424 ppm

Persists for 300–1,000 years in the atmosphere. Released by coal, oil, gas, and cement kilns. Drives 74% of total warming.

CH₄ · Methane GWP = 28×

1,930 ppb

Traps 80× more heat over 20 years than CO₂. Emitted by organic food landfills, livestock, and natural gas pipeline leaks.

N₂O · Nitrous Oxide GWP = 273×

337 ppb

Remains in the stratosphere for 114 years. Driven by synthetic agricultural fertilizers and industrial chemical production.

F-Gases · HFCs/SF₆ GWP = 1,400×+

2% Share

Leaks from air-conditioning refrigerants and electrical switchgear. Replacing old AC units prevents super-pollutant leaks.
